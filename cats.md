# CatFishing - Cat Catalog

## 🟢 Common (11 Cats)

### 1. Wowo (`cat_wowo`)
> "The village’s cheerful welcome committee. He organizes shiny bottle caps and believes every fish caught is a reason for a party."

### 2. Eepy (`cat_eepy`)
> "A tiny master of naps who can sleep anywhere. He claims to have visited a secret wool moon, but usually falls asleep before the story ends."

### 3. Muffler (`cat_muffler`)
> "A stylish fashion critic who never takes off his red scarf. He spends his mornings testing the wind to find the coziest spot on the pier."

### 4. Jelly (`cat_jelly`)
> "A wobbly mystery that smells like grape soda. He jiggles with every step and loves hiding in bait buckets to give fishers damp, purple hugs."

### 5. Mochi (`cat_mochi`)
> "A starry-eyed tortoiseshell master of manipulation. He uses his innocent, bright gaze to trick fishers into giving him free snacks."

### 6. Garfield (`cat_garfield`)
> "A cynical orange philosopher who hates Mondays. He is waiting for someone to invent a rod that catches warm, cheesy pasta instead of fish."

### 7. Skelly (`cat_dead`)
> "A theatrical duo running a scam where one fakes death for pity snacks. The 'deceased' one miraculously recovers the moment a fish hits the floor."

### 8. Comix (`cat_comix`)
> "Born with fur as bold as fresh ink, he treats the village like a silent story. He spends his days watching the waves with intense, quiet respect."

### 9. Pana (`cat_boxmeme`)
> "A humble resident who prefers a sun-dried crate over any luxury home. He spends his time checking if every container he finds is 'friend-shaped'."

### 10. Sarge (`cat_battle`)
> "A self-appointed guardian who treats every sunrise like a tactical mission. He keeps his whiskers perfectly aligned while patrolling the pier for seagulls."

### 11. Nanner (`cat_bananameme`)
> "A sensitive soul who wears a banana suit to blend in with fruit crates. He loves counting bubbles and just wants to be a fruit in peace."

## 🟡 Legendary (8 Cats)

### 46. Valery (`cat_valery`)
> "Valery is the legendary Tavern keeper, a figure of pure grace and quiet authority. She doesn't just run the tavern; she presides over it like a serene goddess of the pier. With a refined elegance that makes even the roughest sailors lower their voices, she listens to every word with a depth of attention that makes you feel like the only person in the world. She is the ultimate vault of the village—every secret whispered over a bowl of milk is locked away forever behind her mysterious, knowing smile. To look into her eyes is to realize she knows exactly how your day will end, but she's far too polite to spoil the surprise."

### 47. NyanCat (`cat_nyan`)
> "NyanCat is a celestial adventurer who decided long ago that the ground was far too crowded for his liking. He prefers to spend his time patrolling the upper atmosphere, leaving a shimmering, multi-colored trail behind him—though he’s convinced it’s just a side effect of a very high-quality grooming routine. He treats shooting stars like giant laser pointers and has been known to accidentally knock a planet or two out of orbit while trying to catch a particularly fast comet. To him, the entire universe isn't a vast mystery; it’s just a giant, glowing room full of things to pounce on."

### 48. Bao (`cat_bao`)
> "Bao is the undisputed mastermind of the local market, and he possesses a legendary nose for anything of value. He can tell the difference between a high-quality gold coin and a shiny bottle cap just by the sound they make when he batts them across the floor. He treats every fish stall like his personal office, and while he doesn't officially own any of the shops, he collects a 'protection tax' in the form of the finest tuna scraps. He is a shrewd negotiator who knows exactly how long to stare at a merchant to get a 20% discount on treats, making him the most respected—and well-fed—businessman in the entire village."

### 49. Pusheen (`cat_pusheen`)
> "Pusheen is a legendary cozy specialist who secretly believes she is the world’s most famous 'influencer,' even if her only real followers are the local pigeons. She treats every snack like a viral event, posing perfectly before every bite as if a thousand invisible cameras are capturing her every move. She has mastered the art of looking 'aesthetic' while napping, fully convinced that her round shape is the peak of modern design. She doesn't just live in the village; she’s a tiny, fuzzy celebrity who only accepts autographs in the form of premium catnip treats."

### 50. Deus (`cat_church`)
> "He is the self-appointed golden guardian of the village church, and he takes his divine status very seriously. He spends his mornings finding the exact spot where the sunlight hits the stained glass, ensuring his fur glows with a heavenly light for all to see. He acts as the keeper of the 'streaks,' believing that every day a fisher returns with a catch is a direct blessing from his golden paw."

### 51. Doraemon (`cat_doraemon`)
> "He arrived from a distant era with a pocket full of advanced tech, yet he still struggles with basic concepts like closed doors. He often tries to fix the village's minor issues with overly complex inventions, such as a gravity-defying bowl for cats who hate leaning over. While his mission is to modernize the pier, he usually gets distracted by a passing butterfly before he can actually deploy his teleporting yarn."

### 52. Flame (`cat_flame`)
> "A literal fireball wrapped in heavy chains, patrolling the pier with the stern authority of a volcanic king. He claims to have emerged from the earth's core, yet he spends most of his time guarding the local bakery’s oven to ensure no one steals his 'throne.' While he carries the weight of the underworld on his shoulders, his iron-clad ego melts the moment someone scratches his chin with a metal poker. He refuses to acknowledge his purring, insisting that the vibrating sound is actually a low-frequency tectonic warning to all nearby fishers."

### 53. Mega (`cat_mega`)
> "Mega commands a towering, neon-pink mechanical suit designed for high-stakes pier defense. The pilot is a tiny kitten who barely reaches the joysticks, yet she treats the heavy machinery like a personal jungle gym. She uses the suit's advanced boosters to snatch treats from mid-air and deploys a 'defense matrix' specifically to block other cats from stealing her spot in the sun. Despite its intimidating appearance, the mech is essentially a high-tech mobile bed that keeps its captain warm and safely out of reach of the local dogs."
